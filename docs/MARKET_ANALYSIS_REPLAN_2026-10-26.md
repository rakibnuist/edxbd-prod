## 1. Market analysis

### 1.1 Demand fundamentals — global

| Signal | Data | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Global outbound motion | ~6.9M international students (2024), projected **10M+ by 2030** (studyinternational.com, Aug 2026) | The pool is growing; Bangladesh's own volume share is small but rising |
| Study-abroad market value | USD 225B (2023) → **USD 490B by 2033E**, ~14.2% CAGR (researchandmarkets) | Long runway; agency commission economics remain healthy |
| Destination interest | 3 of the "Big 4" (US/UK/Canada) still top interest, but policy turbulence is pushing students to **regional Asia + Europe** options; Sweden tops academic-reputation perception (KEG State of Student Recruitment 2026) | The "Beyond the Big 4" shift is not a trend piece anymore — it is measurable survey data across 2026 |

### 1.2 Bangladesh source-market fundamentals (ICEF Monitor, Nov 2025 — still current into 2026)

| Driver | Data | What it means for EduExpress |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound volume | 50K+ tertiary students abroad (OECD 2022); recent estimates 100K+ at all education levels (2026 industry reports); governance-quality estimates put BD in the **top 7 sources for the UK** with 12,700 students in-country (HESA 2024-25 via UUK) | A genuinely under-served market versus India (1.2M) and Nepal (110K) — few specialist, compliant agencies for the volume |
| Economy | GDP growth forecast 2026: **4.9%** (IMF, down from 6.4%); inflation **8.7–8.8%** (2025/26); taka **–30% vs USD in <3 years**; 20% US tariff on garments | Affordability pressure is real → scholarship-honesty and BDT cost transparency are now *conversion features*, not decoration |
| Demographics | 49% of 172M under age 24; youth unemployment 11.5% (13.5% among tertiary graduates) | Chronic demand pressure; parents are 50% of the decision unit — the /for-parents page is strategically important |
| Destination mix | Top: US, UK, Malaysia, Canada, Germany, Australia, China; industry commentary records a clear turn to **"regional options like Malaysia, China and Japan"** (ICEF) | China-first is defensible; Malaysia/Hungary/Korea Wave 1 clusters sit exactly on the demand shift |

**Bottom line:** demand is growing, but the buyable audience is value-conscious and trust-scarce. Currency depreciation makes every bdt-converted cost table a differentiator; fraud history in the sector makes written proof the wedge.

### 1.3 Destination demand shift — who is winning 2026-27

| Destination cluster | 2026 status | Opportunity for EduExpress |
|---|---|---|
| China | 380K international students (2024-25); ~10.7K Bangladeshis (2018 census, base since grown) | **Flagship.** Low-IELTS routes, CSC + provincial scholarships, cost advantage vs UK/AU. Our whole proof engine lives here |
| UK | 686K international (–46K y/y); Graduate Route cut to **18 months from Jan 2027**; June 2026 BCA compliance tightening → visa withdrawals surging (ICEF, Oct 2026) | Honest post-2027 rules = a differentiator most BD agencies haven't caught up to. High risk of published misinformation right now — our verified-date system wins here |
| Hungary | Stipendium Hungaricum is the single biggest free-degree hook for BD self-funded alternative seekers | Wave 1 |
| South Korea / Japan / Malaysia / Germany | Regional Asia pushes rising fast; Malaysia popular-for-BD in agent and student commentary | Korea + Malaysia are Wave 1; protect from hype |
| US / Canada | US trust lowest of Big 4, proposed $70K OPT fee, Canada cap 408K permits (2026) | Not in portfolio — do NOT chase |

### 1.4 Buyer-behavior shift: AI search + Bengali

- **46% of students now use AI tools (ChatGPT etc.) during college search, up from 26% in 2025; 18% removed a college from consideration based on AI-surfaced results** (EAB survey, 2026). AI assistants are a real acquisition channel for education.
- We already ship `llms.txt` and llms-compatible static pages — this must graduate from "exists" to "structured": stable facts, verified-date stamps, and clean Q&A blocks, because assistants quote *fact-shaped* content.
- Bengali: the audience reads Bengali (audit + governance docs). One Bengali page ships today (`/bn/study-in-china`). Bengali depth is the biggest single untapped trust lever for parents.

### 1.5 Competitive landscape

| Tier | Players | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Global multi-destination | IDP Education | Office in Dhaka; dominant UK/AU brand; does not own China-BD-specific authority |
| Large BD independents | MACES, Mentors' Education, PFEC Global, Eduko Pathways, GIC, Education@ | ~200+ university networks, documented reputations; none own an "evidence-first transparency" wedge |
| China specialists | Sangen Education (visible SEO for "study in china bangladesh"), Malisha Education, Confidence Global, Ditos, Abroad | Majority of them sell scholarship-forward, some with unverifiable claims; classic faith-based selling |
| MBBS wholesalers | Russia/Georgia/Kazakhstan volume brokers (Kazakhstan/Georgia/Russia low-cost medical lists everywhere) | Direct competition for the MBBS-in-China niche — recognition-first framing protects us |

**Structure:** ~429 agencies in Bangladesh, 61% concentrated in Dhaka, median size 11–50 staff (RevenueBase, Sep 2026). Implication: **loyal-country SEO territory is uncontested**; nobody is publishing Bengali-language, source-attributed, verified China/UK/Hungary/Korea/Finland/Malaysia education content in a consistent format. The strategy holds.

### 1.6 B2B market reality

- Universities and platform recruiters want compliant BD sending partners; recent policy shifts (UK BCA suspension risk, EMGS/MYCAS formalization in Malaysia) **raise** the value of agencies with documented SOPs.
- The `Partnership` model + `/partnership` page already exist but read as BD-only. B2B acquisition will respond to: written partner process (SOP clarity), commission/payment terms in writing, and lead-handling proof — the same trust assets we sell to students.
- Sub-agent networks are the volume channel in this market; they sniff out agencies that pay late or break promises. The B2B offer must therefore be built on our payment-terms rigor, not commission size.

### 1.7 Where the market validates (and stress-tests) the current strategy

| Current plan element | Market verdict |
|---|---|
| Flagship China | Validated — regional-Asia turn is real, cost press makes China cheaper per degree, and no BD competitor has a transparency-grade China resource |
| Wave-1 timing (UK, Hungary, Korea, Finland, Malaysia) | Validated and slightly **accelerated** by UK policy churn and the Asia-regional shift — UK content with post-Jan-2027 rules can lead Wave 1 despite being the "hardest" country, because it is where misinformed competitors will be weakest |
| Evidence-first vs scholarship-hype | Validated — scholarship-scam warnings are now mainstream student content ("10 red flags" genre), meaning the trust marketplace is opening exactly as planned |
| AI-visibility (llms.txt) | Needs upgrade from asset to program |
| Bengali | Biggest gap — plan extends it |

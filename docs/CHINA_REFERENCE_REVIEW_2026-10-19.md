# China reference-site review

Checked: 19 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). Research status: source discovery and feature review; this is not approval of third-party admission claims. The nine user-supplied URLs were opened individually, in the order below. Observations describe the fetched page, not a full audit of the operator or every linked institution.

The review follows the local `eduexpress-content-governance` and `eduexpress-china-seo` skills. Competitor pages help identify useful questions, institutions and interface patterns. Current eligibility, fees, scholarships, deadlines, recognition and visa rules need university or government sources, a checked date and the applicable programme/year before use on EduExpress. No competitor prose or images have been copied into the website.

## 1. MalishaEdu blogs

Source: [malishaedu.com/blogs](https://malishaedu.com/blogs)

Access: readable page returned.

Observed: dated article cards, topic categories, search and three select controls whose labels/options were not exposed by text extraction. Subjects include scholarship routes, Chinese language, rankings, historic national university projects, Bangladesh applicants and the undergraduate academic-proficiency test. Links lead to CSC, immigration, ranking publishers and the official CSCA site.

Use: build an original China resource hub with clear categories and dates. Prioritize admissions preparation, scholarship conditions, language requirements, costs and city life. Verify CSCA requirements against each university's current notice. Article dates or a recent crawl alone do not establish that an admissions statement is current.

## 2. CUCAS

Source: [www.cucas.cn](https://www.cucas.cn/)

Access: HTTPS and HTTP homepage requests returned script/encoded challenge material rather than usable page content. Homepage content could not be reviewed reliably.

Fallback: publicly indexed CUCAS bachelor's search results were readable. Example: [English-taught programme search](https://bachelor.cucas.cn/search?tag=2-70-0-0-2-30000%2C38000-0-0-0-1-0-0-2-0-0-0-0).

Observed in that fallback: city, teaching language, start date, duration and tuition filters; sorting; an application-open toggle; school search and shortlist. Programme rows show start date, duration, application deadline, language and fee period. Examples include Southwest Jiaotong University and Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

Use: programme-aware filters and clearly dated deadlines are valuable. Indexed examples included elapsed 2026 deadlines, so they are leads for official verification, not active offers. Homepage access remains unconfirmed.

## 3. BHE UNI blogs

Source: [bheuni.io/blogs](https://bheuni.io/blogs)

Access: readable page returned.

Observed: featured/recent articles, dated posts, named author links, excerpts and pagination. Content spans admission routes, qualifications, language tests and scholarships across countries. A China MBBS guide is listed.

Use: give each original EduExpress guide an accountable author, review information and related-resource links. Country/topic categories would help China readers avoid unrelated destination articles. A competitor author identity is not evidence of an EduExpress reviewer and must never be imported as one.

## 4. BHE UNI Study in China

Source: [bheuni.io/study-in-china](https://bheuni.io/study-in-china)

Access: readable page returned.

Observed: study routes, audience suitability, academic/language/health/financial preparation, cost factors, scholarships, living in China, work restrictions, graduate choices and application support. Several entry-requirement panels and FAQs organize the content. Two FAQ answers remained placeholders for future scholarship/language information in the fetched page.

Use: cover these student decisions with Bangladesh-specific evidence, complete answers and links to the actual university rules. Provide a real cost breakdown and recognition checks rather than generic affordability statements. Confirm work/visa details using current government guidance. Do not reproduce broad assurances or incomplete FAQ text.

## 5. StudyInChinas English site

Source: [studyinchinas.com/en](https://studyinchinas.com/en/)

Access: readable, long article-feed page returned.

Observed: foundation-to-degree routes, master's study, scholarships, packing, travel, culture and etiquette. The fetched feed includes 2020/21 programme descriptions under 2024 post dates, scholarship/internship guarantee claims, and accommodation prices expressed in rubles.

Use: pre-departure and language preparation are useful topic ideas. These historic network-specific offers and foreign-currency figures are unsuitable as current Bangladesh guidance. Do not import scholarship guarantees, employment promises or universal language thresholds. Each route needs current university confirmation and a realistic renewal/eligibility explanation.

## 6. BHE UNI China-filtered university directory

Requested source: [China-filtered university directory](https://bheuni.io/universities?country_id=bhe-900050485&location=&query=)

Access: exact URL and a simplified country-only query were inaccessible through the research tool. The general [university directory](https://bheuni.io/universities) was readable.

Observed in general directory: two select controls with unexposed options, an institution/keyword input, search button, paginated university cards and links to individual profiles. Cards expose course counts, living costs, a language indicator and currency. Some missing values appeared as `null`, and at least one currency presentation was inconsistent with its living-cost currency.

Use: combine search, clear filters, result counts and direct profile links. Use explicit labels such as programme language instead of a Yes/No language flag. Display an honest unavailable state for missing data. The precise China result set and dropdown option labels were not verified.

## 7. BHE UNI Linyi University profile

Source: [Linyi University profile](https://bheuni.io/university-details/linyi-university-lyu)

Access: readable page returned.

Observed fields: overview, intakes, campus/location, official website, living budget, application fee, language, rankings, scholarships, fee breakdown, documents, accommodation and course cards. The page links to [Linyi University's official website](https://www.lyu.edu.cn).

Data concern: the quick-facts table gives an RMB 100 application fee, whereas the fee breakdown gives CNY 400. These may describe different routes, but the distinction is not established. Course fees and entry scores lack a visible programme/year source. Some empty sections and generic UK-oriented ranking/application labels appear in this China profile.

Use: create dedicated China profiles with official source links, fee periods and programme-specific entry requirements. Linyi is a useful candidate for official research; none of these disputed amounts should be imported as verified current fees.

## 8. MalishaEdu all-universities directory

Source: [malishaedu.com/list/all-universities](https://malishaedu.com/list/all-universities)

Access: readable page returned.

Observed: university-name search; province, tag and intake filters; clear-all control; 73 displayed university records at the time of review. Cards show institution name, location, badges, ranking and enrolment claims. Countdown components displayed dashes in the fetched content. Candidates visible include Yangzhou University, Zhejiang University of Science and Technology, Ningxia Medical University, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Harbin Institute of Technology, Jiangsu University and Tianjin University.

Use: province and intake are useful filters. Verify all badges and institution identities independently. Ranking values need publisher, edition/year and scope; enrolment counts need provenance. Do not copy unsourced rankings, partner claims, enrolment numbers or countdowns.

## 9. Admissions.cn

Requested source: [admissions.cn](http://admissions.cn/)

Access: homepage HTTP redirected to HTTPS, which timed out; an HTTPS retry also timed out. Search-index content was available, and [www.admissions.cn/universities](https://www.admissions.cn/universities) was subsequently opened successfully.

Observed in university directory: A–Z navigation, keyword search, university/profile links, city/province, historical institution facts and programme counts by degree. Examples include Anhui University, Beihang University, Beijing Institute of Technology and Beijing Foreign Studies University. Currentness of those institutional statistics is unestablished.

Observed in indexed [course search](https://admissions.cn/courses/search?keyword=Education): location, degree, language, intake, tuition-band and scholarship filters; programme rows contain duration, start date, level, fee and language.

Use: separate institutions from individual programmes, preserve fee units and expose useful combined filters. Do not treat the operator's application popularity list as an academic ranking. Exact homepage content was not directly confirmed.

## Recommended implementation decisions

These are EduExpress recommendations inferred from the review, not facts endorsed by the reference sites.

1. Maintain an original, Bangladesh-focused China pillar linked to a filterable directory and one canonical profile for each institution.
2. Store institution, programme, intake, fee and scholarship facts separately. A university can offer multiple languages, different deadlines and different fee periods; a matched programme must satisfy combined filters.
3. Provide name/subject search plus province/city, degree, language, intake and fee filters. Include clear-all, active selections, useful empty results, visible result counts and direct profile links.
4. Display programme name, teaching language, duration, tuition currency/period, admission cycle and official source. Mark elapsed deadlines closed and historical notices as historical.
5. Profiles should explain academic fit, eligibility/documents, full costs, scholarship coverage/renewal, campus/housing, language preparation and recognition questions. Add actual official links and checked dates.
6. Treat secondary directories as discovery sources. Do not transfer competitor agency partnerships, author identities, admission claims, student counts, guarantees or unlicensed media to EduExpress.
7. Record unknown facts explicitly and request confirmation from the university before presenting them as current. Keep author/counsellor review status honest; a source lookup is not a completed professional review.

## Research limitations

This review covers the pages listed above and the stated fallbacks. Search-index fallback observations can lag the live page. Select options, visual layout and interactions were not fully inspected. Access failures are recorded rather than described as successful reviews. Candidate universities are research leads, not confirmed EduExpress partners or endorsements of particular programmes.

> - **Where AD Bank Student Files Are Mandatory:** Destinations requiring advance tuition wires or blocked accounts—such as Malaysia (EMGS + university tuition), the UK, Finland (EUR 9,600/yr documented liquid funds), and self-funded European tracks—require opening a Student File at an AD commercial bank branch under Bangladesh Bank GFET rules (typically BDT 5,000–6,000 + VAT opening/renewal fee depending on the bank).

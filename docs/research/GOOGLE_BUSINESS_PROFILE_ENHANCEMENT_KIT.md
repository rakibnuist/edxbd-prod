## 3. GBP Q&A Seed Bank (Post & Answer via Official Owner Account)

Seed these genuine parent/student questions directly in the Google Business Profile Q&A section so Maps searchers get accurate facts before calling:

1. **Q:** Where is the EduExpress International office in Dhanmondi and can parents visit without an appointment?  
   **A:** Our office is at Evergreen Latif, House: 12/1, Road: 4/A, Ground Floor, Dhanmondi, Dhaka 1209. We are open Saturday to Thursday from 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM (closed Friday). Walk-ins and parents are welcome; you can also call +880 1983-333566 beforehand to reserve a counselor slot.

2. **Q:** Does EduExpress charge a file-opening fee or consultancy fee before a China visa?  
   **A:** No. For China, there is no file opening charge and no EduExpress service fee before China visa approval. Any university application fee, embassy fee, medical check, translation, courier, or university deposit required earlier is itemized in writing before you proceed.

3. **Q:** What is the consultancy service fee for Malaysia university applications?  
   **A:** EduExpress charges BDT 0 service fee for partner Malaysian university applications, EMGS Student Pass guidance, and Bangladesh Bank Student File documentation support. University application fees, EMGS charges, medical checks, and tuition are paid directly by the student at official rates.

4. **Q:** Do I need a Bangladesh Bank Student File to study in China?  
   **A:** For full CSC scholarships (where tuition and hostel are waived) and many Chinese universities where partial tuition or dorm fees are paid on campus during registration week, a Bangladesh Bank Student File is usually not required—students travel with standard passport currency endorsement and dual-currency cards. For destinations requiring advance tuition wires (such as Malaysia, the UK, or Finland), our team helps organize the AD bank Student File paperwork.

5. **Q:** Can O-Level, SSC, or Dakhil students apply abroad right after finishing Class 10?  
   **A:** Yes. O-Level students can apply for a 1-year Foundation + 3-year Bachelor in Malaysia (4 years total), and SSC, Dakhil, or O-Level students can explore China’s 3+2 Higher Vocational Diploma + Top-Up Bachelor track (5 years total). Under-18 China applicants must meet MoE Order No. 42 legal guardian verification rules.

6. **Q:** Does EduExpress guarantee university admission or a student visa?  
   **A:** No. Universities, scholarship boards, and embassies make all official admission, funding, and visa decisions. EduExpress checks academic fit, recognition, and document completeness and puts all costs in writing before you apply, but never makes guaranteed-visa claims.

7. **Q:** Do I have to leave my original passport or SSC/HSC certificates as security?  
   **A:** No—students are never required to surrender original documents unless they specifically choose a documented no-deposit custody option with a written receipt, and we never hold original documents for Malaysia or standard assessments.

8. **Q:** Is EduExpress International in Dhanmondi connected to UK-based EduExpress Ltd.?  
   **A:** No. EduExpress International (eduexpressint.com), established in Dhaka in 2018, is an independent Bangladeshi education consultancy and has no affiliation with any UK-registered entity named EduExpress Ltd.

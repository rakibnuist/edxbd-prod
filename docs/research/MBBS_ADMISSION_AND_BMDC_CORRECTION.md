# Medical admission and Bangladesh registration copy

Internal research record: 24 October 2026. These dates and links are not public profile copy.

## Change

Removed the universal instruction to obtain a BMDC Eligibility Certificate before enrolment from the MBBS guide, China hub and the 45 imported medical profiles. Removed BMDC/admission-test records from the Chinese university application-file checklist. University academic entry now stays separate from Bangladesh professional registration.

Public guidance follows: choose an authorised programme, meet its admission rules, complete the degree and clinical training, retain study records, pass the BMDC Registration Qualifying Examination, and complete applicable internship and registration before practice. It does not claim that passing an exam alone is a practice licence.

## Evidence and limits

- Owner-supplied reference: https://csca.app/en/scholarships/countries/bangladesh/clinical-medicine — read in browser. It still asserts an Eligibility Certificate is needed before enrolment and all medical candidates need all three CSCA sciences. Those claims were not copied. Existing university sources show different CSCA combinations.
- Newer official notice: https://www.bmdc.org.bd/news-events/202602000001 — indexed heading says “Notice regarding Eligibility Certificate (10/02/2025)” and listing date is 10 February 2026. Download target is https://www.bmdc.org.bd/upl/doc/news/iCqLeVnNmDcT.pdf. During this review both the live page and attachment redirected to scheduled maintenance. Full notice wording could not be retrieved. Secondary reports describe removal for the 2025–2026 cohort onward, but a universal waiver was not independently confirmed from the attachment.
- Older official RQE checklist https://www.bmdc.org.bd/upl/doc/form/QSPRfvjJ5NtF.pdf lists an Eligibility Certificate alongside MOE recognition evidence. It should not override a later cohort-specific policy. Old source records remain in the CMS and backups.
- Chinese MOE programme rules: https://en.moe.gov.cn/documents/laws_policies/201506/t20150626_191405.html — Article 30 separates English-medium undergraduate medical programme authorisation from general university recognition.
- BMDC internship notice: https://www.bmdc.org.bd/news-events/202605000001 — supports keeping internship and registration in the return-to-practice route.

### 2.2 Bangladesh Buyer Mechanics (Must Be Embedded Across SEO Pages)

1. **Separated Cost Accounting (Anti-Deception Standard):** Never lump tuition, hostel, daily meals, airfare, and agency fees into one opaque headline. Every cost table separates **Tuition & Institutional Fees**, **Living Expenses (Accommodation, Food, Local Travel)**, **Third-Party Statutory Fees (Embassy, Medical, EMGS, Insurance, Notarization)**, and **EduExpress Service Fees**.
2. **Bangladesh Bank "Student File" vs. China Payment Simplicity:**
   - **Destinations Requiring Advance Tuition Wire (Malaysia, UK, Finland, Europe, Australia, US):** Regulated under Bangladesh Bank’s *Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET)* via Authorized Dealer (AD) commercial bank branches. Requires offer letter, official institutional fee estimate, academic transcripts, valid passport/visa, sponsor financials, TM form, single-branch lock, 1-academic-year release limit, and typical bank file opening/renewal fees (BDT 5,000–6,000 + VAT + SWIFT charges).
   - **China Operational Advantage:** Full CSC / Type A awards cover tuition/dorm and pay monthly stipends (CNY 2,500–3,500/mo), requiring zero tuition remittance; many self-funded/provincial-waiver Chinese universities allow remaining hostel/tuition payment **on-campus during registration week** alongside standard passport endorsement—meaning **no Bangladesh Bank Student File bureaucracy is needed** for those China tracks. Highlighting this clearly is a decisive guardian conversion advantage.
3. **Academic Entry Bridges (Bangladesh Curriculum Specific):**
   - **Post-SSC / Dakhil / O-Level (Age 16–18):** Skip 2 years of HSC / Alim / A-Levels (and BDT 4.8L–15L+ in Dhaka A-Level coaching/school/exam fees) via **Malaysia 1-Year Foundation + 3-Year Bachelor (4 years total)** or **China 3+2 Higher Vocational Diploma + Top-Up Bachelor (5 years total)** (checking MoE Order No. 42 legal guardian requirements for under-18 applicants in China).
   - **Post-HSC / Alim / A-Level:** Direct entry into China ranked Bachelor’s (with University/Provincial scholarships + CSCA preparation), Malaysia UK/AU branch campuses (Monash, Nottingham, Heriot-Watt) or 1+2 / 2+1 transfer tracks, UK 3-year Honours, Hungary (Stipendium Hungaricum), South Korea (GKS / direct), Finland (SAT/IELTS routes), and Georgia/China MBBS.
   - **Medical (MBBS) Licensing Reality:** Must lead with **MoE China English-medium authorization**, **WDOMS** listing, and **Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council (BMDC)** Registration Qualifying Examination (RQE) & eligibility rules—never lead with price.
   - **Postgraduate (Master’s / PhD):** CSC Type A/B, GKS, Stipendium Hungaricum, UK 1-year Master’s, Finland research tracks.

## 5. Technical SEO, Schema.org & AI Search (GEO) Specification

Every public market, destination, guide, and Bengali page on `eduexpressint.com` enforces:

1. **Server-Side Rendering / ISR (`revalidate = 3600`):** Full H1, tables, internal links, metadata, and JSON-LD in initial HTML (`curl` verified).
2. **Canonical & Title Hygiene:** Self-referencing canonical on `https://eduexpressint.com/...`; `pageMetadata` enforces `<= 62` char `<title>` with single brand suffix and `120–160` char `<meta description>`.
3. **Bidirectional `hreflang`:** Paired `en-BD`, `bn-BD`, and `x-default` alternates ONLY when complete Bengali equivalents exist (e.g. `/bangladesh-study-abroad-market` ↔ `/bn/bangladesh-study-abroad-market`, `/study-in-china-from-bangladesh` ↔ `/bn/study-in-china`, `/study-in-china-after-o-level-ssc` ↔ `/bn/study-in-china-after-ssc-dakhil`, etc.).

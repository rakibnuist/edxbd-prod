# Study in China hub redesign

Audience: Bangladeshi students and families choosing a university, funding route and practical admission plan.
Primary action: Apply with EduExpress International; preserve country=China.
Idea: EduExpress's programme-specific ClearCost and scholarship support becomes a compact editorial study planner: choose a study level, compare real university profiles, and open the one guide needed next.
Actual assets: existing Great Wall photograph; owner-supplied NUIST campus photograph; brand logo/type/palette; current university records and original source research.
Subject inventory: Bangladesh qualification route; China study levels; bilingual teaching; NUIST atmospheric science; Xi'an Jiaotong preparation; Wenzhou undergraduate choices; CNY and BDT kept separate; Before China/In China ClearCost; university scholarship conditions; no file-opening charge; service fee after China visa approval with third-party exceptions; Dhanmondi counselor desk.

Argument/content deck:
- Study in China from Bangladesh: compare universities, scholarships and costs with EduExpress International; first-fold application CTA.
- Find the answer you need: six distinct topic pages, visible two-sentence summaries, ordinary crawlable links.
- Find your study route: study-level picker changes a real directory search link and its guidance; non-script fallback includes all level links.
- Explore Chinese universities: NUIST, Xi'an Jiaotong and Wenzhou records, accurate locations and genuine logos; no invented partnership/open admission claims.
- Prepare with EduExpress: short application/document/cost/visa support explanation and exact payment wording, links to detailed service policy.
- Four short visible FAQs mirror FAQ schema.

Direction: editorial-report (The printed annual), seed382643293, brand type/palette preserved. The dealt void hero, 88svh sections, full-height CTA and long open FAQ contradict the user's explicit request for a much smaller page. Adapt these to a compact feature spread, 42% editorial axis, actual image band, simple peer-topic index and collapsed FAQ. Keep substantive content on relevant topic URLs rather than creating a long scroll scene. No fake data or new external stock assets. Four-picture and animation quotas also yield to compact usability; real pictures used only where they aid the decision.

Validation: CUA browser for screenshots and interactions; do not use the skill's independent browser inspector. Source lint, TypeScript, SEO/HTTP checks and production build. Current browser API does not offer viewport resizing; do not claim mobile screenshots without a supported UI mechanism.
